**What it is.** The ethnographers' tool for notes and data, used together with Obsidian ([[ethnographic-loop]]). Its participatory mode is the part that mattered in the consent story ([[consent-and-ethics-gate]]).

**What it provides.** Two affordances: lasting, shared records ([[ethnographic-loop]], with Obsidian providing it too) and the participatory consent feature ([[consent-and-ethics-gate]]), which is why the account credits it with clearing the ethics gate.

**Where it differs from Obsidian.** The records are common to both; the consent feature is told only of Telescope. Nothing in the narratives says why both are used.

**Thin.** Two narratives, no account of how it is run or by whom beyond the ethnographers.
