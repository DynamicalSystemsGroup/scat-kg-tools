**Who this is.** A cluster, and the narratives say so themselves. [[members-and-the-data-set]] gathers beneficiary organisations, their members and the participants who enact and resist the loop in field sites, and "clusters all of them" here. [[self-infrastructuring]] clusters Metagov with it, and [[parts-and-deployment]] places those KOI serves here. [[ethnographic-loop]] adds a role without an edge: the community takes part in collecting. The page stands for a role, being served by KOI or taking part in it, and not for one body of people.

**What it holds, across the narratives.** One concern recurs: that the shared data set stays intact, as a club good all participants depend on together ([[forget-incident]], [[members-and-the-data-set]]; [[the-data-set-and-the-two-layers]] states the concern but names no holder, and the page does not supply one). The others each come from a single narrative and concern Metagov in particular: providing its own platforms ([[self-infrastructuring]]), IT that just works with the technical team out of sight ([[self-infrastructuring]]), and, as one contributor appears to have understood it, the right to be forgotten ([[forget-incident]]).

**Where the accounts differ.** The same act is damage to the data set in the team's reading and advocacy in the contributor's ([[forget-incident]]). The page keeps both and chooses neither.

**Thin.** Whether the beneficiary organisations, as distinct from Metagov, hold the self-provision and invisible-IT concerns is not said anywhere. If a later narrative separates the clustered parts, this page may need splitting.
