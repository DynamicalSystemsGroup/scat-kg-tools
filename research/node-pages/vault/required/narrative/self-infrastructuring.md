Members of the [[technical-team|technical team]] came to KOI along parallel tracks. They were concerned about tools that do not interoperate and about vendors controlling not only the data but how it is governed and used, which left data in silos: [[vendor-lock-in|vendor lock-in]]. The infrastructure had been organised around the vendors' external, fragmented needs and not around the beneficiary organisation and its constituents. KOI was put forward as a de-intermediating, interoperable alternative, the [[koi-doctrine|KOI doctrine]], that returns governance of data to the beneficiary organisation.

What made vendor tools untenable differed by use case. At Metagov (here clustered with [[community-members|community members]]) the wish was to [[self-provided-platforms|provide for itself the platforms]] for its sensemaking, decision-making and identity-formation, and it opposed the data-hoovering of web platforms. On the chief engineer's side the commitment was less about escaping vendors and more about avoiding lock-in and keeping data egress possible, a commitment to the doctrine and not to any one implementation.

Uptake was uneven. At Metagov it came late and the first attempts were not really self-infrastructuring. People moved the question of who should serve them elsewhere and wanted [[it-that-just-works|IT that just works]], with the technical team invisible, as vendor tools are. This was the [[koi-pond-chatbot|KOI pond chatbot]], which decayed for lack of participation in maintenance and curation and was decommissioned. Later work, [[ask-metagov|Ask Metagov]], set out more clearly who contributes what and where. It looks more successful, but it is new and people outside the team have not yet used its features.

The two wishes at Metagov, providing for itself and having IT that just works, are both coded and left side by side. Uptake was strongest on the chief engineer's own technical team, which helps provide, maintain and use its infrastructure, including recording, transcription and search; applying the doctrine to vendor tools has allowed migrations that would have been very hard otherwise. No edge is coded for that, since the notes name no separate concern or affordance for it.

Thin spots: the input gives no sequence of dated events, so scenario is the nearest type. It says nothing of what other groups wanted.
