title: "Members lose the thread"
description: "Members drop out of a long-running thread because nothing brings them back to where it stands."
---

Across the trial narratives this is told two ways. In the ethnographers' telling the thread is the study, and members fall away from it between feedback rounds. In the moderators' telling the thread is the forum's own discussion, and the loss is felt as repeated questions. Both agree a short periodic summary helps; they differ on whether it should come as a post or in the inbox. Thin: no member is recorded as saying what they wanted.
