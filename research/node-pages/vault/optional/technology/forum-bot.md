title: "Forum bot"
description: "A bot on the forum that sends messages and posts on a timetable."
---

In the narratives the bot is the thing that does the sending: reminders in one, a posted digest in another. It is never described as deciding what to send; the ethnographers or moderators set the wording and the timing. Where accounts differ is upkeep: one narrative says it needed no attention, another says a moderator restarted it twice. What is thin is who owns it.
