Facilitator's notes, as told. Source: the decision log, D-010, D-017 and D-020, and the mission.

- Workshop participants tell stories in conversations. The work is sized for manual note-taking in Obsidian.
- Facilitators take the raw notes. The setting is Chatham House rule: the people in the data are groups, so no individual is identifiable.
- The vault holds stakeholder groups, and an instance setting caps their number.
- Raw notes carry no attribution. A project that needs it would extend the tool.
- Attribution and anonymity conflict as concerns, and the vault records that as a declared conflict between the two.
