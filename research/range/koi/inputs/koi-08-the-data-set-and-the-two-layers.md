# koi-08-the-data-set-and-the-two-layers

Source: ant-rdf `wiki/Actant-koi--data-set.md`; `wiki/Actant-koi--forget-general.md`; `briefs/koi-architectural-network.md` (characterization of Forget invocation (in-program)); `briefs/koi-ethnographic-network.md` (characterization of the subversive forget invocation)

Field note, architectural view. The KOI data set is the shared knowledge graph the infrastructure accumulates and maintains. It is a club good: its integrity and completeness are something all participants depend on collectively.

The architectural source glosses the operative and constitutive layers only through forget. Its sentence is that a routine forget invocation mutates the data, which the system is designed to permit, but does not change the rules or systems that afford that power, so it does not confuse the two layers. Its effect on the data set is bounded and expected.

The architectural source is silent on the human acts of governance that the ethnographic view foregrounds.

The data set is where the two accounts meet. The architectural source treats routine forget as bounded and expected, and the ethnographic source records a case where forget degraded the shared graph. The sources do not say how the infrastructure should settle this.
