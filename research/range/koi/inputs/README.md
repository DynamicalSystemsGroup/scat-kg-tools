# Range inputs: the public KOI case

These nine short texts are the inputs for the KOI range (WP19). They are predigested material standing in for field notes: each is written as a facilitator's note of what a group does, meets or wants (scenarios, `koi-01` to `koi-06`) or of what the infrastructure is and how its parts relate (architecture, `koi-07` to `koi-09`). They are not real field notes. They were written from existing public coding, so they are a step up from the synthetic lab vault and short of a field test.

Source: the public repository `mzargham/ant-rdf` (https://github.com/mzargham/ant-rdf), commit `a2ec48edc8f44e386eaeada922c4a90349581df3`, the KOI case: `briefs/koi-*.md` and `wiki/*koi*`. Each text has, under its heading, a `Source:` line naming the exact files.

Groups only. The sources name authors, an investigator, contributors and a chief engineer. Here every one appears as a group or role ("the ethnographers", "a contributor", "the chief engineer"). Where a source was thin, the text says so. Nothing is added knowingly.
