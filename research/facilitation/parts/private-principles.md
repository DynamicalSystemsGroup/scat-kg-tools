# Principles from the supplied drafts

The supplied drafts concern a different setting from facilitation sessions, so they bear on the four topics only
indirectly. Paraphrased and unattributed.

- Draw accounts from what people already do; keep them open to the owner's correction or withdrawal (topic a; silent
  on proposed designs).
- A party's account stays its own, and the party decides what travels beyond it (topic b).
- Differences between accounts, and arrangements nobody has looked at lately, are put to people as questions
  (topic c).
- Boiling many accounts down to one can leave a group misled about its own spread of views (topic d).

## What a graph would need to show (capabilities)

- One concern held by several parties, each party's account kept distinct.
- Differing accounts shown together, with the difference left in place.
- Matters left open, not resolved.
- Where and when each contribution came from, and who has confirmed it.
- What a contributor agreed to share outward.
- Change over time.
