  One note-taker per session, someone other than the facilitator (me). Note-taker to be named before each session.
  In disallowed mode the note-taker writes no name.

Shape of each evening (Planning page, "A one-off online discussion" and "An in-person meetup"):
  In person. Sign in by group, never by name. A table per group, a board for what comes up out of turn.
