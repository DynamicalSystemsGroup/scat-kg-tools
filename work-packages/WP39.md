## Purpose

Two rulings, one package (the chief engineer: "Yes, one package").
- **D-205:** the "Linked beyond the text" list is retired. The chief engineer (typed by the agent): "concepts are
  either part of the narrative or they are not. it seems like adding lots of other links is an easy way to make
  clutter ... i would discourage link dumping at the end". A concept is in a narrative only if its account names it.
- **D-191 and RS8:** telling a coder when candidate links carry no evidence, held to "make the right way the easy way"
  and "as simple as possible but no simpler". RS8's readout (`research/candidates/readout.md`) recommends its option
  (e): a candidate is drawn and listed only where the account links both its nodes; no flag; one line counting the
  rest when there are any.

## Goals

- **WP39-G1. The list is retired.** The page contract, the rule catalogue, the coding guide's step, the example
  narrative `saturday-at-the-lending-desk` and the template's narrative no longer describe or hold a "Linked beyond
  the text" list. `check` warns on a narrative with a list of links outside its account, saying to name the node in
  the account or leave it out.
- **WP39-G2. Candidates show evidence only** (subject to O1). A candidate is drawn and listed only where the
  account's prose links both its nodes. The no-account flag is replaced by one line counting the pairs not drawn,
  when there are any. The fingerprint and layered views, their drawings and tables, the CLI, the API and the server
  agree.
- **WP39-G3. The pages say the right step.** The analysis and coding guides say what a candidate means and that the
  next step is to read the account and, if it tells the edge, add the row; a pair the account does not link is not a
  reason to add a row.

## Non-goals

- The rule-4 candidate itself in `graph.py` (unchanged).
- A stricter "same paragraph" test (RS8: it trims 21 of 40 evidenced candidates; an account can tell an edge across
  paragraphs).
- The glossary (WP38 defines "account" without the list).

## Open decisions

- **O1.** Adopt RS8's option (e) now, or after RS8's held assistant readings in Claude and inference.coop (RS8's R3 is
  so far met on the PM's own reading only).

## Definition of good enough

WP39-R1 to R5 met.

## Definition of good

A coder reading a figure is led to the account, never to a row the account does not tell; a narrative holds only what
its account names.
