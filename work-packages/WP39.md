## Purpose

The chief engineer (D-205, typed by the agent): "concepts are either part of the narrative or they are not. it seems
like adding lots of other links is an easy way to make clutter ... i would discourage link dumping at the end". A
concept is in a narrative only if its account names it. Held to "as simple as possible but no simpler" (D-208), this
also answers RS8's question (D-191): when every node of a narrative's table is linked in its account, candidates come
only from the account, and each one has the account behind it, so no flag or counter is needed.

## Goals

- **WP39-G1. The list is retired.** The page contract, the rule catalogue, the coding guide, the example narrative
  `saturday-at-the-lending-desk` and the template's narrative no longer describe or hold a "Linked beyond the text"
  list.
- **WP39-G2. One rule.** Every row end of a narrative's `Edges told` table is linked in its account: a warning while
  the narrative is a stub or draft, an error at `done`, like the other row-end rules.
- **WP39-G3. Candidates come from the account.** A candidate is a pair of nodes the account links, in adjacent
  layers, that no row joins. The `no_account` flag and its rendering are removed.
- **WP39-G4. One sentence** in the coding guide: name a node in the account, then record its row.

## Non-goals

- Anything that replaces the flag (no counter, no footer line).
- A change to the document format's version (the removed key was added today, before v0.1).
- The glossary (WP38 defines "account").

## Open decisions

None. The shape was decided by the chief engineer (D-208).
