## Purpose

Give R-20 its range level: real but predigested material, the public KOI case in `mzargham/ant-rdf`, coded there with
the ANT ontology, rebuilt as a SCAT model. It stands in for field notes, a step up from the synthetic lab vault and
still short of a field test. Words follow `GLOSSARY.md`.

## Goals

- **WP19-G1. The range material**: from the KOI case's public contents, a set of field-note-like source texts (the
  inputs), with groups, never named people (Agent's defaults).
- **WP19-G2. The range vault**: those texts drafted into narratives and coded in the current model (one page per
  narrative, "Edges told" tables), done by hand through the tool, as R-19 requires a person could, by workers acting as
  coders. A vault that checks clean, with its views generated.
- **WP19-G3. A short note** on what translating ANT coding into SCAT kept, lost and needed interpreting, for the chief
  engineer and for the coding guide.

## Non-goals

- The live chat runs on this vault (WP18-R4 and WP20).
- Any change to the tool or the ontology; any material that is not public.

## Requirements

| ID | Requirement | Checked by |
|---|---|---|
| WP19-R1 | Everything comes from the public contents of `mzargham/ant-rdf`, cited; no person is named (groups only). | A reviewer's read and grep |
| WP19-R2 | The vault checks clean, every narrative is `done`, and the views generate. | `scat check`; the view calls |
| WP19-R3 | A fresh reviewer reads a sample of narratives against their source texts and agrees the coding is a fair reading. | The review record |

## Definition of good enough
