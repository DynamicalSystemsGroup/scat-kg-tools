## Purpose

In the whole-vault layered drawing, nodes in alphabetical order make lines cross, two unrelated islands are interleaved,
and nothing shows which narrative tells an edge; the assistant said it "can't reliably trace individual lines across
that fan by eye" (`acceptance/walkthroughs/analysis-in-practice.md`).

## Goals

- **WP35-G1. Fewer crossings.** Nodes within each column are ordered to reduce crossing lines.
- **WP35-G2. Islands apart.** Parts of the graph that share no node are drawn as separate bands.
- **WP35-G3. Who tells an edge.** In the layered drawing an edge can be marked by the narrative that tells it, with a
  legend.

## Non-goals

- The views' data; new kinds of drawing; interactive drawings.

## Definition of good enough

WP35-R1 to R5 met.

## Definition of good

A coder can follow every line in a whole-vault picture by eye.

## Agent's defaults

- *(default)* Ordering by the barycentre heuristic (simple, deterministic); no new dependency.
- *(default)* Edge marking is an option, off by default; marks by line style or colour safe for colour-blind readers.
