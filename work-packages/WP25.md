## Purpose

Give a reader a clear choice of coding path and a walkthrough with screenshots for each (C14, MISSION G14 as amended by
D-148), and rethink the facilitating and planning pages together so they serve the capture of field notes (C15). Both
candidates are quoted in full in `work-packages/candidates.md`. Words follow `GLOSSARY.md`; every page follows D-139
and D-146 (no prompt residue, no test or work status).

## Goals

- **WP25-G1. Coding paths, a drill-down.** The navigation's coding section opens on `docs/coding-paths.md`, which sets
  out the four paths and when each fits, then one walkthrough per path: **by hand** (`docs/coding.md`, no AI);
  **an AI chatbot** (`docs/chat-path.md`, example Claude), with **leave no trace** as its option for a strict data
  policy (`docs/leave-no-trace.md`, example inference.coop); **an AI IDE** (`docs/ide.md`, example Codex); **a local
  LLM** (`docs/local-llm.md`, example Ollama running in Docker, or a better local model if the spike finds one). Each
  walkthrough names its provider in a `provider` field at the head of its page; the chatbot, IDE and leave-no-trace
  walkthroughs use different providers.
- **WP25-G2. Screenshots and the fewest clear steps.** Each walkthrough shows a screenshot, with alt text, at each point
  a person looks or chooses; it has as few steps as stay clear. No automation is added in this package (`src/` and
  `deploy/` are untouched); a step a script could remove is noted for later.
- **WP25-G3. Facilitating, narratives first.** The facilitating section opens with `docs/narratives.md` (what a
  narrative is, how field notes become one), then Scenarios and Architectures as heuristics for organising and
  interpreting narratives, then `docs/facilitate-timeframe.md` (accounts of what was and of what could be: the
  narrative's timeframe), with attribution as the vault's attribution mode sets it (WP23). All aimed at capturing field
  notes.
- **WP25-G4. Planning by event.** `docs/planning.md` gives light, high-level guidance by kind of event (a one-off online
  discussion, a recurring discussion, an in-person meetup, a multi-day workshop): how to set the stage for facilitation
  and field notes, who takes field notes, and, when several people take them, how the notes are collected and compiled
  as input to coding. Choosing the attribution mode stays in the set-up stage.

Candidates: WP25 takes C14 and C15 (D-137). It touches C12 (WP24, via `docs/coding.md`: WP25 edits no node-page lines)
and C13 (the attribution modes, taken by WP23).

## Non-goals

- New tool features; any change to `src/` or `deploy/`.
- Bloat: no path or event kind gets more than one page; the planning page stays within its word budget.
- Choosing or recommending a provider.
- The chat-path page's Docker Desktop box and its test (`tests/docs/test_chat_guides.py`): WP26's sweep moves that status.

## Definition of good

A reader sees at once the ways to code, picks one that fits their data policy and tools, and follows it with pictures;
an organiser plans an event so the field notes come in ready to code.

## Definition of good enough

WP25-R1 to R8 met. The walkthrough runs that need the chief engineer's accounts (Claude, Codex, inference.coop) run in
his sitting, with their screenshots taken there; until then those pages are written from the providers' own docs and
corrected from the run.

## Agent's defaults

- *(default)* Existing file names are kept; `docs/coding-paths.md`, `docs/local-llm.md`, `docs/narratives.md` and
  `docs/facilitate-timeframe.md` are new.
