## Purpose

The chief engineer (D-168, typed by the agent): "make sure our glossary is consistent and aligned with our app and
workflows ... i don't want the glossay to be huge and there are terms from a few different levels of abstraction in
there, this glossary is for the user of the app (which is the organizer deciding scope and policies, planning and
soliciting contributors, facilitating during events, gathering field notes after the event, coding those notes,
analyzing, and eventually reporting out findings."

RS6's readout (`research/glossary/readout.md`, accepted D-171) found:
- 59 words, 41 inconsistencies (2 high), and 25 user-facing words the glossary lacks;
- `terms.md` at its 910-word test cap;
- an evidence rule (R2) giving a core of 32 organiser words;
- four ways to organise the result, costed at 3 to 8 units.

## Goals

- **WP38-G1. A small glossary for the organiser.** The words an organiser meets across their work, at one level of
  abstraction, each defined briefly and consistently.
- **WP38-G2. The other words have a home.** Assistant and builder words go to the contracts or the architecture page.
  Redundant words are dropped or folded into another entry.
- **WP38-G3. The pages agree with it.** Each of RS6's 41 inconsistencies is fixed or recorded as accepted, with its
  reason.
- **WP38-G4. The terms page and its tests follow.** `docs/reference/terms.md` mirrors the glossary. The test keeps
  checking the ontology names and the mutation terms, with a lower cap.

## Non-goals

- The stage words themselves (WP29 defines them; this package uses them).
- The ontology's identifiers.
- New docs content beyond fixing a word's use.

## Open decisions

- **O1. How the glossary is organised.** RS6's options:
  - (A) by the organiser's journey, or by WP29's stages: 5 units;
  - (B) the model first, then the work, then policy: 5 units;
  - (C) a short core plus a "for builders" page: 8 units;
  - (D) one alphabetical list: 3 units.

## Definition of good enough

WP38-R1 to R5 met and O1 resolved.

## Definition of good

An organiser can read the glossary in a few minutes and then meet no word in the pages, the tool or the assistant's
replies that means something else.

## Agent's defaults

- *(default)* The core is RS6's R2 set (32 words) plus WP29's stage words. Of RS6's 25 "needs an entry" words, those
  with 6 or more pages are added.
- *(default)* The `terms.md` cap is set at the cleaned page's size plus 10%.
- *(default)* History (decisions, acceptance records, scopes) keeps the words it used.
