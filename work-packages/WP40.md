## Purpose

The chief engineer (typed by the agent): "i need you to rethink more than just this rule. i am worried that you are
missing the whole point of this kind of coding exercise and that you have introduced. unnecessary and undesirable rules
and machinery. do an audit across the repo, explain the ontology, the architecture and the features of our app. then
justify them on the basis of how it helps draw out abstract patterns from concrete narrative. keep in mind our 2
heuristics: "make the right way the easy way" and "as simple as possible but no simpler"; clarify what parts of what we
built provide the syntax, the semantics, and the pragmatics for SCAT."

And, on the point of the exercise: "the coders job is to identify patterns, label those patterns and organize the data
according to them. we provide a meta-model, tools, and guidance for that. ... we're not trying to add detail we're
moving the other way, identifying abstract patterns in the concrete accounts."

## Goals

- **WP40-G1. Explain what we built.** The ontology, the architecture and the features of the tools and the app, in
  plain terms, sorted into syntax (what a well-formed vault is), semantics (what its parts mean) and pragmatics (how a
  person uses it to get from concrete accounts to abstract patterns).
- **WP40-G2. Justify or flag each part.** Every rule, view, tool, contract term and feature gets one verdict: keep
  (with the reason it helps draw out abstract patterns), simplify (what to cut back), or cut. The two heuristics are
  the test.
- **WP40-G3. Several perspectives, verified.** Peer reviewers with different personas each do the audit (G1, G2)
  independently. Only after all their reports are in, the ACE compares and contrasts them, verifies their claims
  against the repository, and writes one synthesized report that points back to each reviewer's report.

## Non-goals

- Making any of the cuts or simplifications. They become work packages after the chief engineer decides each one.
- New features or designs (C22 included); the audit may say where they fit.
- The pilot project or anything private.

## Definition of good enough

## Definition of good

After reading it, the chief engineer can see what each part of SCAT is for, and what to cut, to reach a releasable,
usable state.

## Agent's defaults

- The chief engineer (D-217): the reviewers audit first; the ACE does no audit of its own until their reports are in,
  then compares and contrasts them, verifies their claims and writes the synthesized report.
- *(default)* A Sonnet PM runs the reviewers in parallel and collects their reports; it writes no synthesis.
- *(default)* Four reviewer personas: a qualitative researcher who codes field notes (method fit), a first-time
  organiser using the chat path (usability), a minimalist maintainer (cuttable machinery), and a knowledge-graph
  modeller (ontology). Each gets the same brief (G1, G2) and no one's conclusions.
- *(default)* Reviewers read only; nothing in `src/`, `contracts/` or the docs changes.
- *(default)* Paths as in the deliverables table.
