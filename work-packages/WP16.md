## Purpose

Bring the ontology, the tool, the template and the docs to the mission as merged (0e74f19): one page per narrative
whose status is its coding state; nodes and edges as base types; edges that name the narratives that tell them;
fingerprints; relationship annotations in place of `conflicts`; links and edges held in step; and an Obsidian layout a
coder can work in. It builds on the approved architecture (`contracts/architecture.md`, modules M0 to M10) and uses
the words of `GLOSSARY.md`.

## Definition of good

A coder new to SCAT opens the template in Obsidian, codes a narrative in place, and sees the tool name every mismatch
with a fix that works; the ontology is no bigger than the mission needs.

## Definition of good enough

WP16-R1 to R9 met, and the ACE has walked a coding session in Obsidian on the migrated template.

## PM's build-stage defaults (answers to the open slots, on D-088; ACE to confirm at acceptance)

These are details inside D-088, marked *(default)*. Unit workers build to them; WP16's architecture unit records them in
`contracts/architecture.md`.

- **Types.** Page `type` values: `group`, `concern`, `affordance`, `technology` (the node types, glossary words),
  `narrative` (with an optional `kind` of `scenario` or `architecture`, and the existing past/future/mixed mark), and
  `index`. The TBox keeps formal identifiers (Heldby, Addresses, Attributes) labelled with the plain words.
  *(default)*
- **Edge storage.** An edge is no page and no field: it is one row of a narrative's "Edges told" table, and the
  knowledge graph holds one edge per (type, from, to), naming every narrative with a row for it. The edge's type is
  fixed by the layers of its ends: `holds` (group, concern), `is addressed by` (concern, affordance), `is provided by`
  (affordance, technology); the CLI and API spell these `holds`, `addressed-by`, `provided-by`. *(default)*
- **Table reading.** The loader finds the first table under the heading `Edges told` (any level, case-insensitive),
  with columns type, from, to, note; it trims and collapses whitespace in every cell, accepts `\|` inside a cell and
  an alias link `[[x|text]]`, and accepts the verb or the slug in the type cell. *(default)*
- **Link and fingerprint.** A narrative's links are every link on the page, the table's included. Its fingerprint is
  its linked nodes plus the edges its rows tell. So rule 3 (R-23) reduces to: a row end that is not a link, or not a
  node, is a mismatch (warning on `stub` or `draft`, error on `done`); rule 4 stays a candidate, with no message.
  Rows that duplicate a pair within one narrative, tell a pair in the wrong direction or between non-neighbouring
  layers, or carry an unknown type are conformance problems with a fix. *(default)*
- **Edge status.** Derived: `done` when every narrative with a row for it is `done`; no edge sign-off. *(default)*
- **Operations.** `record_edge(type, from_, to, told_by, note="")` and `remove_edge(from_, to, told_by=None)`; CLI
  `scat record-edge TYPE FROM TO --told-by NARRATIVE [--told-by ...] [--note TEXT]` and `scat remove-edge FROM TO
  [--told-by NARRATIVE ...]` (without `--told-by`, every narrative that tells the pair). Recording stubs a missing end
  or narrative, adds a row only where none exists ("unchanged" otherwise) and leaves links; removing deletes rows and
  leaves links. Both refuse when a narrative they would change is `done`, naming the coder's step (set it to `draft`).
  `stub_pair` is removed. *(default)*
- **`done`.** Setting a narrative to `done` grades the page as it would stand at `done`, and refuses on any error,
  with the problems and their fixes. *(default)*
- **Relationship annotation.** A line in the body of one of the two entries, with a link to the other. It has no
  meaning to the tool. The `conflicts` field, `conflictsWith` and `SCAT-CONFLICT-SELF` leave; the template's values
  migrate to such lines. *(default)*
- **Index pages.** One Obsidian Base per type, generated once by `scat index` into `index/<type>.base` (a view of the
  pages whose `type` matches), so there is nothing to go stale: `SCAT-INDEX-STALE` and the class pages leave.
  Obsidian 1.9 or later. *(default)*
- **Schema.** `schema_version` becomes 2 (page types and statuses rows change; record-edge and remove-edge results
  added). *(default)*
- **Integration.** Units merge into the PM's integration branch (`pm-work`); `dev` receives it once the full suite is
  green, so a red intermediate state never reaches CI on `dev`. *(default)*
