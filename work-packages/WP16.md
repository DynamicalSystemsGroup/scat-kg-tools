## Purpose

Bring the ontology, the tool, the template and the docs to the mission as merged (0e74f19): one page per narrative
whose status is its coding state; nodes and edges as base types; edges that name the narratives that tell them;
fingerprints; relationship annotations in place of `conflicts`; links and edges held in step; and an Obsidian layout a
coder can work in. It builds on the approved architecture (`contracts/architecture.md`, modules M0 to M10) and uses
the words of `GLOSSARY.md`.

## Goals

- **WP16-G1. The ontology and contracts** (G1, R-13): the ontology file, page contracts, rule catalogue and machine
  interface for the new model, with the slots the architecture left to WP16 decided (edge as page or field, type
  slugs, where an edge records its narratives, record-edge and remove-edge names, the one-link-away scope). The
  machine interface's `schema_version` is bumped by its rule (R-16). User-visible names follow the glossary (D-081).
  Builds on M0 (ontology reader), M1 (loader), M2 (result and fix data) and M3 (checks).
- **WP16-G2. The layout** (G15, R-21): decided by a spike that builds two or three candidate layouts of the same lab
  vault on `spike/layout`, which the ACE reviews in Obsidian with screenshots before the decide stage. Decides how M1
  reads entries (page or field) and how M10 lays the vault out.
- **WP16-G3. Links and edges** (G17, R-23): mismatch detection over R-23's closed list with a fix for each; recording
  and removing an edge (G4); `done` refused while the check fails, with the page judged as it would stand at `done`
  (architecture M5); rule 4 as D-085 sets it. Builds on M4 (mismatch), M5 (operations) and M7 (Python API and CLI).
- **WP16-G4. The docs**: the coding guide rewritten for the coding loop and for incremental coding, breadth first and
  depth first (G17); the terms page rewritten against `GLOSSARY.md`, its drift test checking both the glossary and the
  ontology (D-081); the flow page and the data, rights and consent page with its "Before you start" checklist, as
  `WP13.md` sets out (its goals and requirements carry over, read with the mission's one persona, the organiser, and
  the journey J1 to J10); the architecture document linked from the docs site. Documents M3 to M5 and M7 for
  the coder.
- **WP16-G5. The template**: the worked example and the example vault migrated by a one-off (D-081), the case study
  coded in the new shape, both pins moved in order (the WP8 and WP11 pattern). M10.
- **WP16-G6. Carried items**: the prior-art readout's guidance-versus-example gap as a test case (D-085); play-test
  findings F-1, F-3, F-5 and F-7 where their files are touched.

## Definition of good

A coder new to SCAT opens the template in Obsidian, codes a narrative in place, and sees the tool name every mismatch
with a fix that works; the ontology is no bigger than the mission needs.

## Definition of good enough

WP16-R1 to R9 met, and the ACE has walked a coding session in Obsidian on the migrated template.

## Blast radius

As `work-packages/impact/WP16.md` section 5 sets it, plus `docs/coding.md`, `tests/ops/test_ops_status.py` and the
fix-harness tests (D-085), `spike/layout`, and `research/layout/`. Not `MISSION.md`, `GLOSSARY.md` (a glossary change
would be a separate logged call), `research/` outside `research/layout/`, or accepted scopes.
