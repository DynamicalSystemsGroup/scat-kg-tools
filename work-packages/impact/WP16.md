Built by the change consultant (unit WP16-U0, `MISSION.md` R-18, G13) before the WP16 scope is approved. It reads the
scope as `work-packages/plan.md` item 4 describes it and `MISSION.md` G1, G3, G4, G15, G17, R-21 and R-23 (Amendment 5)
set it. Words follow `GLOSSARY.md`. Swept at `origin/dev` 1dc76ab, `origin/main` 0e74f19, `origin/spike/analysis`
448a012 and `origin/spike/obsidian-plugin` 727052d in `scat-kg-tools`; `origin/dev` 5a7290f and `origin/main` a63b8c6
in `scat-kg-template`. Nothing was written to the template checkout.

## 1. What changes (in glossary words)

- **One page per narrative.** The paired page types (raw and coded, for scenario and for architecture: four of the
  twelve page types) become one narrative page whose `status` is its coding state (`stub`, `draft`, `done`); scenario and
  architecture are its subtypes, and the past, future or mixed mark stays on it. The raw/coded back-link rules go with the
  pairs.
- **Nodes and edges as the two base types.** The four node types and the three edge types sit under them. An edge is its
  own entry between two nodes in neighbouring layers, at most one per pair, and it **names the narratives that tell it**.
  Today the three edge types are relation pages (`heldby`, `addresses`, `attributes`), each with a class page.
- **A link is never an edge.** A narrative's link to a node says only that it refers to it. A narrative's
  **fingerprint** is the nodes it links plus the edges that name it; it is **valid** when every edge naming it has both
  nodes linked by it.
- **Relationship annotations replace `conflicts`.** The optional `conflicts` field on concern pages, the `conflictsWith`
  property and the rule `SCAT-CONFLICT-SELF` leave; existing values migrate. A relationship annotation joins two entries of
  one type, has no meaning to the tool and stays out of every view.
- **Mismatch detection (R-23).** The closed list of forms that must agree (a narrative's link to a node, an edge's entry,
  an edge's references to its nodes and its narratives) is checked; each **mismatch** is reported with a fix the coder
  can apply; severity is a warning while the narrative is a draft and an error once it is `done`; an edge cannot be
  `done` unless every narrative it names links both its nodes. Today's partial cover is `SCAT-MENTION`, `SCAT-RAW-BACKLINK`,
  `SCAT-REL-DUPLICATE` and the link rules.
- **Record and remove an edge (G4).** New operations on the Python API and the CLI with their machine-readable results
  (R-8, R-16); they replace `stub_pair` and `scat stub` for relation pages.
- **The Obsidian layout (G15, R-21).** Each narrative one page, nothing duplicated, no pages that carry no content of
  their own; the design decides which other entries are pages and which are fields, so it decides how index pages and
  stubs are realised. Today: 12 folders, seven generated class pages and a generated table in each.
- **Absorbs WP13.** `docs/flow.md` and `docs/data-and-consent.md` (new), one-line links in `docs/index.md` and
  `docs/planning.md`, and the Chatham House passages in the home, planning and two facilitation guides.
- **The terms page** (`docs/reference/terms.md`, WP12) is rewritten against `GLOSSARY.md`, and its drift test
  `tests/docs/test_terms.py` with it.
- **Both repositories.** The template's worked example (35 pages), its Obsidian configuration and its pin to the tools
  change; the tools' docs test clones the template at a pinned commit, so the two pins move in a stated order.

## 2. The sweep

Run in bash (not zsh). `TOOLS` is any checkout of `scat-kg-tools`, `TEMPLATE` any checkout of `scat-kg-template`;
only `fetch` and `git grep` over refs are used, so no working tree is read or written. The groups: **A** the raw/coded
page types and their rules; **B** `conflict` (every form: `conflicts`, `conflictsWith`, `field-conflicts`,
`SCAT-CONFLICT-SELF`); **C** the relation page types and their words (`heldby`, pairs, relation, `SCAT-REL-`, the
TBox properties `involves` and `coded`); **D** `fingerprint`, `timeframe` and the past-or-future mark (new words
and a field that moves page); **E** class pages, index pages, `indexes`, Obsidian and its configuration; **F** the
link and edge rules, mismatch, backlink, entail, `stub_pair`, `scat stub`, `edge`; **G** WP13 and the terms page
(Chatham House, the flow and consent page names, `reference/terms`, WP12, WP13). **P** the two repositories'
pins on each other. **N** file names. **STALE** is the strict old-word list that must be empty outside the kept and
historical paths at acceptance.

## 8. Open questions (for the ACE; not decided here)

1. **Order against WP15.** Plan item 2 forbids detailed design in any further package before the architecture document
   is approved; item 4 stages WP16 as spike, decide, build. May WP16's spike stage start before WP15 is approved?

   relationship annotation, and the index pages are all unsettled; the layout (R-21: no duplicated content, no pages with
   no content of their own) decides, in Obsidian itself.

   slug with a subtype field; whether the TBox class names `Heldby`, `Addresses`, `Attributes` stay as the edge types'
   names; the machine interface's `schema_version` bump and what it moves.

   formal names.

7. **Operation names.** The names, arguments and REPL and CLI twins of record-edge and remove-edge (R-8), and whether
   the old `stub_pair` is removed or kept as a thin form.
8. **What the terms page's drift test compares.** `tests/docs/test_terms.py` ties the page to the TBox today; the rewrite
