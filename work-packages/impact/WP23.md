## What changes

A vault declares one **attribution mode** (done, U2). A new page type `contributor`, outside the four layers, holds the people a narrative is **attributed** to; a narrative's field `attributed_to` is a list of links to those pages. Contributor pages are in the **knowledge graph** (its sense widens) but not the **layered graph**: no edge, no cap, no fingerprint/edge table/layered view/trace. One rule per mode (required SHALL name; disallowed SHALL NOT, an error at every status; optional MAY), worded identically in the catalogue, `docs/norms.md` and `contracts/assistant.md`. A view `scat attribution` (CLI, Python, MCP). The assistant's rules take the deontic pass (C11).
