## Purpose

Give the architecture document (WP15) and the ontology-and-layout package (WP16) the lessons of earlier work, read as
a way of thinking, not as parts to carry over (`MISSION.md`, Design guidance, "Prior art"). Words follow
`GLOSSARY.md`.

## Goals

- **WP14-G1. The readout.** `research/prior-art/readout.md`, at most 1,800 words, answering five questions, each with
  what the sources show, what to keep, what to leave, and a recommendation traced to a mission goal or requirement:
  1. How did earlier work split the work between a human operator and an AI assistant, and how was authority kept with
     the human? (For the three actors, G14, R-19.)
  2. How was model, view and control separated, and what loops ran between people, AI and the model? (For G16.)
  3. How were knowledge-complex vaults laid out in Obsidian: which entries were pages and which fields, how links and
     edges were kept in step, and what made a vault navigable or not? (For G15, G17, R-21, R-23.)
  4. How was verification by the machine kept apart from validation by a person? (For the coding loop and R-1.)
  5. What went wrong, or was abandoned, and why? (For the builders' heuristic.)
- **WP14-G2. Sources and citation.** A source table at the end: one row per public source, cited, with what was read,
  and a single row "earlier private work", read for principles only, never quoted or named (Governance,
  constraints).

## Sources

Public: *Formalizing Document Assurance* (INCOSE IS 2026; the PM's brief gives the local copy), `mzargham/ant-rdf`,
`DynamicalSystemsGroup/knowledgecomplex`, `BlockScience/assurances-audits-accountability` (an Obsidian vault).
Private, principles only: earlier private work, which the ACE names to the PM in the brief and which is never named
in this repository. Client vaults are not available locally and are not read.

## Non-goals

- Any change outside `research/prior-art/`, the board and the metrics log.
- Designing the architecture or the layout; the readout recommends, WP15 and WP16 decide.
- Viewing vaults in Obsidian; the ACE does that in WP16, with full-screen control. Workers read the files.

## Definition of good

The readout changes what WP15 and WP16 would otherwise do: each recommendation is specific enough to act on, and the
builders' heuristic is visible in what it says to leave behind.

## Definition of good enough

explicitly marked as context.
