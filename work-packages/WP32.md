## Purpose

The chief engineer (D-172): "a fingerprint thats keyword flagged as an architecture doesn't get a stakeholders (group)
column and a fingerprint that is keyword flagged as a scenario doesn't get technologies. the default interpretation is
the generic narrative that gets all 4 columns. i also think the plotting methods should have the word group swapped for
stakeholder so the for columns match S, C, A, T". In the runs, both chat assistants read an architecture's lone group
and a scenario's empty technology column as gaps (`acceptance/walkthroughs/analysis-in-practice.md`).

## Goals

- **WP32-G1.** A fingerprint drawing of a narrative with `kind: architecture` has no stakeholder column; with
  `kind: scenario`, no technology column; with no `kind`, all four. Nodes of a dropped column, if the narrative names any,
  are not lost: the drawing says how many were left out and why (the kind).
- **WP32-G2.** Every drawing (fingerprint, layered, trace) labels its first column "stakeholder", so the columns read
  stakeholder, concern, affordance, technology.

## Non-goals

- The views' data (`views.fingerprint` and the others keep every node; only the drawing follows the kind).
- Renaming the `group` type, page folders, edge names or glossary words (the glossary cleanup decides words).
- Layered and trace drawings by kind (they mix narratives; all four columns stay).

## Definition of good enough

## Definition of good

A coder reads a fingerprint's empty space as meaningful: missing columns are the kind's, gaps in shown columns are the
coding's.

## Agent's defaults

- *(default)* SVG and PNG follow the same rule (one drawing, two formats).
